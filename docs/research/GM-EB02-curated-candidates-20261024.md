## Selection requirements
Learning objective: a motivated novice can keep a steady pulse while playing with fingers rather than a pick, a recognisable musical phrase, and practical bass/melody coordination. Previous three AI-original drafts were explicitly musically rejected. Owner decision on 2026-10-24 **explicitly permits different meters** in repertoire and sees diagnostic value in them. The original 4/4-only requirement in #77 is superseded. Meter must remain authentic and the metronome/diagnostic must support or clearly declare limitations.

## Musical-review flow
1. Play the linked MIDI or an independently authorized recording and review exact score. Have an actual guitar-playing person assess melodic interest, phrasing, difficulty and correct right-hand technique.
2. Record owner decision per individual selection: SELECTED_FOR_ADAPTATION / REJECTED / NEEDS_MORE_REVIEW.
3. Time signature is not an exclusion criterion. For the Waltz preserve its 3/8; assess beginner suitability and arrange a truthful metronome count/accents before assigning the lesson.
4. After approval: use licensed MusicXML/LilyPond source from Mutopia (when available) for a versioned guitar score and TAB conforming to MUS-NOTATION-01; validate all notes, voice timing and audio.
5. Keep content hidden from production/automatic assignment until musical, rights and notation validation all pass.

## Product design implications
The selected repertoire may be cataloged as its own canonical work; avoid silently pretending a copyrighted or pre-existing composition is an AI-original `GM-EB02`. Catalogue metadata should represent composer, editor, source URI, edition license, arranger where applicable, score license, audio license, work-versus-exercise identity and skill tags. Track implementation outside this research-only PR if schema changes are necessary.
