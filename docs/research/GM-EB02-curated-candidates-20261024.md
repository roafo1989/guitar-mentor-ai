## Musical-review flow
1. Play the linked MIDI or an independently authorized recording and review exact score. Have an actual guitar-playing person assess melodic interest, phrasing, difficulty and correct right-hand technique.
2. Record owner decision per individual selection: SELECTED_FOR_ADAPTATION / REJECTED / NEEDS_MORE_REVIEW.
3. If owner selects the Waltz, explicitly decide whether a beginner PULSE exercise can be in 3/8; otherwise research specifically **4/4**, not a disguised meter substitution.
4. After approval: use licensed MusicXML/LilyPond source from Mutopia (when available) for a versioned guitar score and TAB conforming to MUS-NOTATION-01; validate all notes, voice timing and audio.
5. Keep content hidden from production/automatic assignment until musical, rights and notation validation all pass.

## Product design implications
The selected repertoire may be cataloged as its own canonical work; avoid silently pretending a copyrighted or pre-existing composition is an AI-original `GM-EB02`. Catalogue metadata should represent composer, editor, source URI, edition license, arranger where applicable, score license, audio license, work-versus-exercise identity and skill tags.
