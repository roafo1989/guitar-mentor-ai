Original E-blues fingerstyle miniature. Version **1.0.1**; 12 bars, **4/4**, **88 BPM**, standard EADGBE tuning. License **CC BY 4.0**; source **ORIGINAL**. Musical acceptance and guitar playability: **PENDING_OWNER**.

## Musical form

Bars 1–4 E7–E7–A7–E7; bars 5–8 A7–A7–E7–C#7; bars 9–12 F#m7–B7–E7–E7. The C#7 is a secondary dominant to F#m7. On each beat the thumb alternates bass root and fifth; the upper melody deliberately uses offbeat entries and rests. Last bar holds the tonic E as the ending over continued bass.
